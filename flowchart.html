PAMOJA CMG Loan Tracker · How it works

The loan journey, from registration to the last repayment

The whole journey on one page: four parties, eight chapters, one shared record. Click any chapter or box to see that chapter on its own, with its explanation alongside.

TAMISEMI authorises lenders→ Councils verify groups and appoint lenders→ Groups meet, save and borrow→ Lenders decide, disburse and confirm repayments→ Everyone sees the same status

Based on the PAMOJA CMG Loan Tracker beta (September 2026). Quorum, share limits, the 30-day instalment cycle and the 120-day delinquency threshold are system settings the Administrator can change. Paper documents named here are confirmed as received in the app; the paper itself stays with the office that holds it.
